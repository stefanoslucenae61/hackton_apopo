# Meeting with Parci (PM, element61)

## Core Message from Parci
The demo must answer one question for the board: **why does clean data matter, in euros and patients?**
Not shiny tech — monetary value. The headline target is **20% fewer errors** by December.

## What the Demo Must Show

- **Lead with the "why" of migration**, not the how. The business needs to feel the pain of dirty data before they'll fund the fix.
- Quantify data quality: show concrete metrics (e.g. % records with missing lab results, conflicting thresholds, zero temperatures) pulled from real APOPO data.
- Make it actionable: show APOPO *exactly* what needs to change in their data, not just that something is wrong.
- Anchor every claim in evidence from the data — no opinions.

## Key Questions to Address in the Demo

- What metrics quantify the current data quality gap?
- What is needed for a migration to be technically possible?
- Will it be a full migration or phased? (Lift & shift vs. greenfield vs. hybrid)
- What are new capabilities APOPO could unlock once data is clean?
- What does success look like at each step, with a timeline?

## Migration Framing

- Ask APOPO for their migration templates (lift & shift, greenfield, etc.) to anchor the conversation.
- Explore **Agentic Migration**: AI that doesn't just move data but cleans, reconciles, and validates it as it migrates — this is the differentiator.
- Reverse-engineer from December success: what must be true in the data by then?

## C-Level Framing (CEO likely in the room)

Two outcomes only:
1. **Making more** — more TB cases detected, more donor confidence, path to FDA 98% approval
2. **Costing less** — lower cost per case, fewer manual corrections, less analyst time on fire-fighting

Frame cost as: *what does APOPO's data problem cost them today, every day?*
